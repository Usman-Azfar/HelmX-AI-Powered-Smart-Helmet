# v1.1.0 publication note — October D4/K1 review

`README.md` and `GITHUB_ARTIFACTS.md` in this folder are the original v1.0.0 records and are kept
byte-for-byte. Read them with this correction:

- This archive contains the completion review (`README.md`), the aggregated results and paired
  analyses (`summary.json`) and the operator attestation for the October D4/K1 studies.
- The raw captures described in those records are **not** in this archive: the full six-pair
  studies, the earlier failed K1 attempts, the smoke tests, the per-recording scheduler traces
  (about 199 MiB each before compression), scripts and source snapshots. They are retained in the
  author's private evidence archive. The earlier failed K1 attempts remain labelled failed and are
  not pooled with the successful six-pair study.
- `compressed_artifact_manifest.json` records how the scheduler traces were losslessly compressed
  for archiving (original and compressed byte counts and SHA-256 hashes). Its paths refer to the
  private archive layout, not to files in this archive.
- The I²C diagnostic of the failed K1 attempt is published in `../i2c_diagnostic_20261011/`.

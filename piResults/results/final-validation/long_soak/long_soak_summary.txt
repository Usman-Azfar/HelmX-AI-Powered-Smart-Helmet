Memory over 3 hours: first-quarter mean 48.86 MB -> last-quarter mean 54.32 MB
(+5.46 MB net, slope +0.041 MB/min). The script's own automated verdict labels
this "stable plateau" -- reporting the actual numbers rather than just the

projected per 3hr at this slope), not perfectly flat. Not large enough to be
alarming over a 3-hour run and no exceptions/failures resulted, but should be
described in the paper as "no leak large enough to matter over 3 hours" rather
than "provably zero drift" -- an 18-hour+ run would be needed to distinguish a
genuine slow leak from noise at this magnitude, which is out of scope here.

Cross-reference with E2/E3's finding: this is the third independent run on the
deployed POLLING method (after E1 and E4) to show ZERO reconstruction-only
near-threshold triggers, over the largest window count yet (33,734, vs
E1's 1,975 and E4's 3,881) -- while both FIFO-based runs (E2, E3, 20 min each)
showed 5 each. This strengthens, without proving, the hypothesis in
acquisition_compare/acquisition_comparison.txt that the FIFO/decimation
acquisition path (not just "idle input + INT8 quantization" alone) is a
contributing factor to that finding -- three separate long polling runs,
zero occurrences; two separate FIFO runs, five each, every time.
